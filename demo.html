How this differs from the production model. The browser demo runs the per-payer denial × Beta-GCR factor without the lag-curve convolution — i.e., it answers “how much will this month's billing realize across all future periods?”, not the per-period forecast the production model produces. The point of the demo is intuition for the payer-mix lever; for the full time-series forecast read the methodology.

All math runs in your browser — see assets/demo.js. Beta sampling via Marsaglia–Tsang Gamma; quantiles from a sorted trial array. Constants from config/adjudication_params.yaml.
